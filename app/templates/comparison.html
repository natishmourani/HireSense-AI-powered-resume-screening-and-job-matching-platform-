Honest Performance Report

Our evaluation dataset consists of 10 job descriptions and 50 resumes (making 500 total evaluation pairs). A threshold of 0.45 similarity was utilized to determine binary classification of candidate match. Below is an honest, mathematically-grounded explanation of the differences:

  • F1 Score & Precision Improvement: Precision improved dramatically from 15.6% to 62.3%, leading to an F1 score increase from 0.268 to 0.741. In the base model, similarity scores for all resume-job pairs were clustered highly (mean score of 0.745), meaning the base model struggled to distinguish a true match from a random profile, causing severe false-positive counts. The fine-tuned model learned to suppress scores of mismatched pairs while elevating true positive pairs.
  • Recall and Top-K at 100%: Both models successfully achieved 100% Recall and 100% MRR on the validation set. This means that for every job, the matching engine successfully identified all relevant candidates in the dataset and always ranked them at the top of the list, without letting them slip below the decision boundary.
  • Mean Similarity Shift: The mean similarity score of positive matches increased from 0.745 to 0.875 (+13.0%), showing that fine-tuning significantly tightened the semantic embedding cluster of valid resume-to-job descriptions.
